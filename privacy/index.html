Flyaway Sim – Morne Coaching: Privacy Policy

Version 1.1.1-beta · Effective November 8, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what the App needs to run your coaching: your name and email, your TrackMan practice data, your bookings, the notes and messages between you and your coach, and — if you turn on notifications — a device token so we can send them.
  • Your coach and the small team that runs the App can see your data. Nobody else can.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never track you across other apps or websites.
  • We never see your TrackMan password.
  • You can see, correct, download or delete your data at any time. Deleting your account is in Settings.

This summary is for convenience. The full policy below is what applies.

1. Who we are

The Flyaway Sim – Morne Coaching app (the "App") is run by Flyaway Sim Golf ("Flyaway," "we," "us"), 1371 Raiders Way, Suite 150, Henderson, NV 89052. This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It works alongside our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. Your name and email address. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. We don't use passwords.
  • Messages and voice recordings. Notes, messages and voice messages you send to your coach.
  • Bookings. Lessons you book or cancel, and your lesson package balance.
  • Agreements. A record that you accepted our Terms of Service: the version, the date, and which device and app version you used.
  • Support requests. Anything you send us when you ask for help.

Information from TrackMan, with your permission

  • When you connect your TrackMan account, the App fetches your practice sessions: dates, clubs and shot data (for example ball speed, launch, spin, carry, club path and face angle).
  • You sign in on TrackMan's own page. We never see or store your TrackMan password. The App keeps a TrackMan sign-in session on your phone only, so it can stay connected.

Information created when you use the App

  • Coaching records. Notes, drills and voice messages from your coach, and whether a session has been reviewed.
  • Sync and technical information. When a sync runs and whether it worked, your device type (iPhone or Android), and the App version and build number. We use this to find and fix problems.
  • Notifications. Reminders, such as when TrackMan needs you to sign in again, are created on your phone. If you turn on notifications, we collect a device notification token — an identifier from Apple or Google for your device — and use it only to deliver Morne's messages (such as a new note or announcement). Apple and Google deliver those notifications to your device. We don't use the token for advertising or to track you, and you can turn notifications off in the App (Settings) or in your phone's settings; turning them off removes the token.
  • Crash reports. If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports with us under Apple's own privacy policy.

What we don't collect

  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera, health data or browsing history.
  • Card or bank details. The App doesn't take payments.
  • Advertising identifiers. The App has no ads and no ad tracking.

3. How we use it

  • To run the App: show your sessions and progress, keep TrackMan connected, and deliver your coach's notes.
  • To give you coaching: your coach reviews your sessions and replies.
  • To manage lessons: bookings, cancellations and package balances.
  • To keep the App secure and working: sign-in, preventing misuse, finding and fixing bugs.
  • To contact you about your account: sign-in codes, invites, booking messages and important changes. These are service messages, not marketing.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to build a profile of you for anyone else.

4. Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Your coach. Your coach sees your sessions, bookings, notes and messages. Coaches can't see students who aren't theirs.
  • The people who run the App. A small number of people who build and support the App for Flyaway can access data when needed to fix problems or answer your requests.
  • Service providers. These companies host and run parts of the App for us and may process your data only to provide their service:
  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (United States).
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes, invites and other app emails.
  • Expo (EAS): builds the App and delivers App updates.
  • Apple and Google: distribute the App, deliver push notifications to your device (Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging), and, for test versions, provide crash reports.
  • TrackMan. The App connects to TrackMan only to fetch your own sessions. We don't send your App data back to TrackMan.
  • When the law requires it. If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.
  • If the App changes hands. If the App is transferred to another company, such as one that takes over running it, your data goes with it under this policy.

We require our service providers to protect your data at least as well as this policy does. We never sell your personal information.

5. Tracking across apps and websites

We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't allow third parties to collect information about your activity over time and across other apps or websites through the App.

6. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your data so the App works.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, sessions, TrackMan data, notes, messages and voice recordings within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed within a further 30 days.
  • What we keep after deletion: a record that you accepted our Terms and when your account was deleted, and anything the law requires us to keep. Your coach and Flyaway may keep their own business records of lessons and payments outside the App.
  • Disconnecting TrackMan stops new syncing. Sessions already in the App stay until you delete your account. To have a single session removed, email us and we'll delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See or download your data. Email info@flyawaysimgolf.com and we'll send you a copy.
  • Correct it. Ask your coach, or email us.
  • Delete it. Use Delete my account in Settings, or email us. Removing the App from your phone doesn't delete your account.
  • Disconnect TrackMan at any time in Settings.
  • Turn notifications off in the App (Settings) or in your phone's settings. Turning them off in the App removes the device token.
  • Nevada residents: we don't sell covered information. You can still send a request about the sale of your information to info@flyawaysimgolf.com.
  • Residents of other states with privacy laws, such as California: we honor requests to know, correct or delete your information wherever you live.

We'll respond within 30 days and won't treat you differently for making a request.

8. Children and teens

  • The App is not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.
  • For users aged 13 to 17, a parent or legal guardian must accept our Terms. A parent or guardian may ask to see or delete their child's information at any time by emailing info@flyawaysimgolf.com.

9. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Access controls in the database mean each student can see only their own data and each coach only their own students.
  • Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your email, so there's no password to steal.
  • Your TrackMan sign-in session stays on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as Nevada law requires.

10. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. The App is intended for users in the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make a material change, we'll update the effective date and tell you in the App before the change applies. The current version is always available in Settings and at the link on our App Store and Google Play listings.
